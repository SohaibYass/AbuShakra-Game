Competition Rules

Version: 2026-06-19  ·  Placeholder — adjust prize/eligibility terms before launch.

How to take part

  1. Register a public display name and your birth month/year, and accept these rules and the privacy policy.
  2. Play a run. Each registered run is tracked online.
  3. Your best run is ranked on the public leaderboard.

Eligibility

  • Players under 13 may not register without separate verifiable parental consent.
  • Offline runs (when you decline registration or the server is unavailable) are not ranked.
  • One profile per player. Runs flagged as suspicious may be reviewed or disqualified.

Scoring & ranking

Players are ranked by their best qualifying run, using this exact order of tie-breakers:

  1. Most carabiners collected (total_carabiners, highest wins).
  2. Then most gear collected (total_gear: shoes, rope, helmet).
  3. Then most levels (peaks) completed.
  4. Then fastest completion time — and a fully completed run always ranks above an incomplete one.
  5. Then earliest finish (whoever achieved it first).

Fair play

Final totals are calculated by the server from accepted level checkpoints — not from values sent by the browser. Negative, duplicated, or impossible statistics are rejected, and impossible completion times or excessive carabiner counts mark a run for review. Attempting to tamper with submissions may lead to disqualification.
