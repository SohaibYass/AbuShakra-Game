AbuShakra Adventure offers an optional online competition leaderboard. This page explains what we collect when you choose to register, why, and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Public display name — shown on the leaderboard. Choose any name; it need not be your legal name.
  • Birth month and birth year — used only to determine an age group. We do not ask for the full date of birth.
  • Gameplay statistics — carabiners collected, gear collected, levels completed, completion status and time.
  • An anonymous account identifier — created automatically so your runs can be linked to your profile. No email or password is required.

How we use it

  • Your display name and game statistics are shown publicly on the leaderboard.
  • Your birth month/year is private. It is stored only to compute an age group (under_13, 13_17, 18_plus) and is never shown publicly.
  • We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Age requirement

Players under 13 cannot register for the competition without separate verifiable parental consent. You can still play the game offline without registering.

Data storage

Online data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) backend with row-level security so that players can read only their own private profile. The public leaderboard query returns only display names and game statistics — never birth information or account ids.
